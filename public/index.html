      📋 PATCH NOTES

      v3.0.0 — "OFFICE EXPANSION"
      New System Daily Coin Economy
      🪙
      Daily Coin Allowance
      All players now receive 5 coins each day upon login. Coins are tied to your username and persist across sessions. Balance resets every midnight Riga time — whether you spent them or not. Use them wisely.
      RESETS: 00:00 EET / EEST
      ⚡
      Boost Vote — Spend 1 Coin for an Extra Skip
      Found a song so terrible you simply cannot wait for your colleagues to agree? Spend 1 coin to add an extra vote toward the skip threshold. Multiple players can boost simultaneously — enough boosts can tip a vote over the majority line without anyone else clicking Skip.
      COST: 1 COIN PER BOOST
      New Feature Song Reputation System
      ▶
      Play & Skip Counter
      Every song now tracks how many times it has been played and how many times the office has voted to remove it. Stats are displayed live under the song title and persist permanently — so that one song everyone keeps skipping will have a permanent record of shame.
      STORED IN: SUPABASE DATABASE
      👍
      Like & Dislike Voting
      Each time a song appears, players can cast one Like or one Dislike. Changing your mind is allowed — clicking the opposite reaction switches your vote. Clicking the same button again removes it. Votes are tracked per song appearance, so you can vote fresh each time.
      ONE VOTE PER PLAYER PER APPEARANCE
      📊
      Live Stats Bar
      A compact stats row now appears under every song showing total plays, total skips, total likes and total dislikes — all time, across every session, every day. This data survives server restarts and does not reset.
      New Game Office Blackjack — Coin Gambling Expansion
      🃏
      Blackjack Table is Open
      A fully functional Blackjack game is now accessible via the card button on the right panel. Standard rules apply: dealer hits on soft 16, stands on 17+. You can Hit, Stand, or Double Down. Winnings and losses update your coin balance in real time.
      ACCESS: 🃏 BUTTON · RIGHT PANEL
      💰
      Bet Your Coins
      Set your bet before each hand. Win and receive your bet back plus the same amount in coins. Hit Blackjack (Ace + 10-value on deal) and earn 1.5x your bet. Lose and the coins are gone. Going bust on a doubled bet hurts twice as much — you have been warned.
      BLACKJACK PAYS: 1.5x
      New Feature Volume Voting
      🔊
      Vote to Change Volume ±5%
      Two new vote buttons below the skip button let the office vote to nudge volume up or down by 5%. Same majority rules as skip. 30-second cooldown after each change. Volume display stays in sync with Spotify automatically every 15 seconds.
      COOLDOWN: 30 SECONDS
      New Widget Holiday Calendar
      📅
      Public Holidays for 15 Countries
      A mini calendar sits at the bottom of the screen showing public holidays across Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Netherlands, Germany, Belgium, UK, Ireland, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Norway and Sweden. Hover any highlighted date to see which countries observe it. Navigate freely between months.
